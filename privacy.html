Magic List Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Magic List: To Do & Reminders (package com.dn.magic.todolist), published on Google Play by Bharat Product House ("we", "us"). It explains what the app does with information. In short: Magic List does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Magic List has no account or sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. The release version of the app does not even request the Internet permission.

2. Information stored on your device

The tasks, lists, notes, steps, reminder times, focus-timer history and settings you create are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through Magic List, and we cannot see or access it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If you use Android's own device backup, Google may back up this app data as part of your device backup under Google's privacy policy; you control that in your device settings.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show task reminders, alarms, the optional daily plan and the focus-timer alert.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): optional, so reminders can ring on the exact minute.
  • Run at startup: to re-arm your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for alarm and reminder vibration.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data.

4. Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. If you choose "Share all tasks as text", the app hands the text to the Android share sheet and you choose where it goes.

5. Children

Magic List is a general productivity app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal, non-rooted device.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
